# BIT HAVEN TRADEMARK & BRANDING POLICY

Version 1.1

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## 1. PURPOSE

BitHaven encourages the community to build, fork, modify, study, and
redistribute software associated with BitHaven.

This policy exists to distinguish independent community projects from
official BitHaven projects and to protect the BitHaven name and identity from
misrepresentation.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

"BitHaven" means BitHaven LLC, a New York limited
liability company, and its authorized representatives.

## 3. BIT HAVEN MARKS

"BitHaven", "Bit Haven", BitHaven logos, official project names, domains,
graphics, symbols, and other identifying marks ("Marks") may constitute
trademarks, service marks, or protected branding of BitHaven.

## 4. PERMITTED REFERENCES

You may truthfully state that:

* your project is based on a BitHaven project;
* your project is a fork of a BitHaven project;
* your software is compatible with a BitHaven project; or
* your software was developed using a BitHaven project.

Such statements must be accurate and must not imply official affiliation.

## 5. FORKS

Independent forks may use their own project name, logo, domain, and branding.

A fork should not use "BitHaven" as its primary product or organization name
in a manner that would reasonably cause users to believe the fork is an
official BitHaven project.

Examples of acceptable descriptions include:

* "Community fork of BitHaven [Project]"
* "Based on BitHaven [Project]"
* "Independent fork of [Project]"

## 6. OFFICIAL STATUS

Only projects authorized by BitHaven may represent themselves as:

* official BitHaven projects;
* maintained by BitHaven;
* operated by BitHaven;
* endorsed by BitHaven;
* sponsored by BitHaven; or
* affiliated with BitHaven.

## 7. LOGO USE

The BitHaven logo may be displayed when accurately identifying an official
BitHaven project or BitHaven itself.

Using the logo on an independent fork, commercial product, service,
organization, or website requires permission when such use could imply
official affiliation or endorsement.

## 8. COMMUNITY PROJECTS

BitHaven welcomes independent community projects inspired by or derived from
BitHaven software.

Independent projects are not required to obtain permission merely because
they interact with, fork, or build upon BitHaven software, provided that they
comply with the applicable software license (the BitHaven Community License
or BitHaven Open Source License, as applicable to that project) and do not
misuse BitHaven branding.

## 9. NO IMPLIED ENDORSEMENT

Use of BitHaven software, documentation, or publicly available information
does not create a partnership, sponsorship, employment relationship,
endorsement, certification, or other affiliation with BitHaven.

## 10. ENFORCEMENT

Use of the Marks in violation of this policy is not authorized. BitHaven may
request that noncompliant use be corrected or discontinued, and reserves all
rights and remedies available under applicable trademark law if noncompliant
use continues after notice.

Violation of this policy by a licensee of the BitHaven Community License or
BitHaven Open Source License may also constitute a material breach of that
license.

## 11. PERMISSION

Requests for permission to use BitHaven branding outside the ordinary
permissions of this policy should be directed to BitHavenLLC@Gmail.com.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of New York, USA, without regard to its
conflict-of-laws principles.

another person or organization.
